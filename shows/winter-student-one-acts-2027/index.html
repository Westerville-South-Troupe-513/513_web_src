About the Show

Winter Student One-Act Plays

The Winter Student One-Act Plays place directors Lydia Battles, Charlie Nickoli, and Lucas Lusher at the helm of their own short-form productions. Individual titles will be announced as the program develops, with each director guiding a company through the choices that give a story its voice, rhythm, and theatrical life.

One-act theatre moves quickly, making every entrance, image, and line count—and giving audiences several different experiences in a single visit. Join us for an evening that showcases student collaboration, imaginative staging, and the next wave of Troupe 513 storytellers.
