About Troupe 513

Creative Team

Meet the directors and theatre professionals who guide Troupe 513 productions.

Director

Matt Wolfe

Wolfe has been the theatre director at South since 2013. In addition to being South’s theatre director, Wolfe teaches IB Theatre at South, and Intro to Theatre and Acting at all three Westerville high schools.

Vocal Director & Pit Director

Tyler Domer

Tyler has been the music director and pit director at South since 2018. He is also the Staff Accompanist at Worthington Kilbourne High School, Accompanist at Church of the Master, and provides piano lessons and voice coaching.

Choreographer

Cindy Straub

Cindy has been the choreographer at South for many years. In addition to working at South, Cindy operates a dance studio where she teaches all ages with a focus on musical theatre.

Costume Director

Katie Smith

Katie started as the costume designer at South in 2023 after helping with costumes for four years. Her day job is the attendance clerk at South.

Booth Director

Seth Sprang

Seth is one of the troupe’s technical directors, with an emphasis on sound and lighting. He has been at South since 2022 and works as a video and podcast editor and live-events technician for OhioHealth.

Scenic Director

Chris Tucker

Chris is one of the troupe’s technical directors, with an emphasis on set design. He has been at South since 2021 and also freelances for events, creates 3D models, and works as a stagehand at the McCoy Center.
