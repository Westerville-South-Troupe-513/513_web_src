Help Troupe 513 students create ambitious productions, build lasting skills, and share unforgettable live theatre with our community.

Your Gift Brings the Next Story to Life

Every production begins with an idea, but bringing it to the stage takes materials, equipment, professional guidance, and a community willing to invest in student artists. Donations to the Westerville South High School Theatre Boosters help turn those ideas into memorable experiences for students and audiences alike.

What Your Donation Makes Possible

Sets, Props & Costumes

Your support helps purchase lumber, paint, hardware, props, fabric, costumes, and the many details that transform an empty stage into another world.

Music, Movement & Creative Guidance

Donations help us work with pit musicians, choreographers, costume designers, and other artists who expand what students can learn and accomplish.

Scripts, Lighting & Sound

Productions depend on performance rights and scripts as well as lights, microphones, batteries, and dependable technical equipment that allow every story to be seen and heard.

A Stronger Troupe Community

Gifts also support the traditions that make Troupe 513 feel like a family, including Theatremas, the ice cream social, the awards ceremony, company meals, and snacks during long rehearsals and show weekends.

When you donate, you are helping students learn to perform, design, build, lead, solve problems, and rely on one another. Thank you for helping the next production—and the next generation of theatre students—step into the spotlight.
