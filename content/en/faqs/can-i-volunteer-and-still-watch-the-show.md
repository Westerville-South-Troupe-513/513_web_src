title: "Can I volunteer and still watch the show?"
category: "Volunteering"
order: 140
featured: false
active: true
---
Often, yes. Ticket scanners and most box-office volunteers finish shortly after the performance begins, while concessions and raffle volunteers generally help before curtain and again during intermission.

Some roles may require you to miss a few minutes or remain in the commons for part of the performance. Each signup will explain the timing so you can choose the role that works best for you.
