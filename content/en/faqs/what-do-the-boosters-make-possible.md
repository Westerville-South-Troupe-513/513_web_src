The Boosters help turn the theatre program's plans into memorable productions and student experiences. Their support includes:

- **Show-week support:** Company meals and snacks, concessions, ticket operations, raffles, and practical help that keeps a busy production running.
- **Student experiences:** Theatremas, the ice cream social, awards, celebrations, and traditions that create a welcoming troupe community.
- **Access and opportunity:** Scholarships, financial assistance, conference travel, and educational opportunities that help more students participate fully.
- **Fundraising and partnerships:** Donations, sponsorships, community partnerships, and special events that keep student theatre supported and sustainable.
