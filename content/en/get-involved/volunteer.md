Main-stage productions rely on adult and family volunteers for concessions, ticket scanning, box-office support, raffles, company meals, and other show-weekend needs.

Show-specific volunteer signups are typically shared through BoosterHub shortly before each production. Join the Boosters and watch BoosterHub announcements so you receive each opportunity when it becomes available. Many jobs begin about an hour before curtain and still allow volunteers to see most or all of the performance.

It is especially helpful when families volunteer for productions in which their own student is not participating. That spreads the work across the season and lets show families focus on their students.
