The Boosters bring families, alumni, and community supporters together around a shared purpose: giving Westerville South students the resources, encouragement, and experiences they need to create ambitious live theatre.

We are an independent nonprofit organization serving the theatre program at Westerville South High School in Westerville, Ohio. Students participate as performers, directors, designers, and crew members; families and community volunteers support the work that happens around each production.

### What We Support

Our fundraising helps provide the materials and expertise productions depend on: sets, props, costumes, scripts and performance rights, lighting and sound supplies, and guidance from musicians, choreographers, and other artists. Volunteers also welcome audiences, work concessions, and help provide company meals during busy rehearsal and performance weeks.

The program includes main-stage plays and musicals alongside student-directed full-length productions, one-act evenings, and original work. These experiences give students opportunities to practice collaboration, creative problem-solving, and leadership. [Explore the current season](/shows/) or [look back at past productions](/shows/archive/).

### How You Can Help

Attend a performance, [volunteer your time](/volunteer/), or [make a donation](/donate/). Local businesses can also [become sponsors](/sponsors/). Membership and show-specific volunteer communications are managed through BoosterHub; our [Contact page](/contact/) connects you directly with the Boosters if you need help getting started.
