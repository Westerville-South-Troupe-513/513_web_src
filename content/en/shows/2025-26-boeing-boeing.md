long_summary: |
  Bernard is happily engaged to three airline hostesses—American Gloria, Italian Gabriella, and German Gretchen—each unaware that the others exist. With help from his bewildered housekeeper Berthe, he relies on international flight schedules to ensure that only one fiancée is in Paris at a time.

  Bernard's system begins to collapse when a new generation of faster aircraft disrupts the timetables and all three women converge on his apartment. Joined by his visiting friend Robert, Bernard races to keep doors closed, stories straight, and his relationships from colliding in Marc Camoletti's fast-moving comedy of timing, deception, and escalating chaos.
