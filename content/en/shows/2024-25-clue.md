long_summary: |
  Six strangers arrive at the isolated Boddy Manor under familiar aliases: Miss Scarlet, Professor Plum, Mrs. White, Mr. Green, Mrs. Peacock, and Colonel Mustard. Their unusual dinner party takes a deadly turn when their host is murdered, leaving Wadsworth the butler to guide a house full of suspects through hidden motives, mounting bodies, and a dangerous assortment of weapons.

  Based on the classic board game and the 1985 film, Sandy Rustin's stage adaptation turns the search for the killer into a rapid-fire blend of mystery, physical comedy, and escalating chaos. Every door, clue, and accusation brings the guests closer to an answer—and gives the audience another reason to suspect everyone.
