long_summary: |
  After a whirlwind summer romance, Danny Zuko and Sandy Dumbrowski unexpectedly reunite as seniors at Rydell High. Danny is torn between the feelings he shared with Sandy and the cool reputation he maintains with his friends, while Sandy tries to find her place among the confident Pink Ladies.

  Jim Jacobs and Warren Casey's musical brings the class of 1959 to life through energetic dancing, teenage friendships, and a rock 'n' roll score featuring favorites such as "Summer Nights," "Greased Lightnin'," and "We Go Together." *Grease* follows its students through the excitement and uncertainty of growing up while asking how much anyone should change to belong.
