  Sophie has grown up on a Greek island with her independent mother, Donna, but she longs to know the identity of her father before she gets married. After finding Donna's old diary, Sophie invites three possible fathers to the wedding without telling anyone which question she hopes one of them can answer.

  When all three men arrive, Donna is forced to confront a past she thought she had left behind while Sophie discovers that family cannot be solved like a puzzle. Built around the songs of ABBA, *Mamma Mia!* brings mothers, daughters, old friends, and unexpected romances together in a sunlit celebration of love and belonging.
