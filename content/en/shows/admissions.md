  Joshua Harmon's award-winning play refuses easy answers as it turns one family's crisis into a fierce conversation about race, power, opportunity, and hypocrisy. Sharp, timely, and often startlingly funny, Admissions invites audiences to recognize themselves in the gap between what people believe and what they want.
